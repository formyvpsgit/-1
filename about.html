About the project

Ruya: clearer planning
for a stable financial life

A shared educational hackathon project that puts AI to work for the financial literacy of citizens and residents.

The idea

Born from a real need

Many young people and families in the UAE want to save or invest, but don't know where to start — and a licensed financial advisor is usually expensive. "Ruya" bridges that gap with an AI-powered analysis engine that understands the context of the UAE economy and turns a few simple numbers into an actionable plan within seconds.

The goal is not to give licensed investment advice — it's to raise financial awareness, steer personal liquidity toward organized local channels, and avoid the common mistakes that lead to debt.

What does Ruya offer?

  • Instant analysis of income and commitments with a 50/30/20 split
  • An emergency-fund plan calculated in AED
  • Realistic local investment channels with risk indicators
  • Practical monthly steps with estimated saving power
  • No sign-up, and no storage of your financial data
